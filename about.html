The Foundation

Precision. Protection. Personality.

Three words. One mission.

AURAFX began in 2018 in a small workshop in Sector 8, Rohini. The founder, a trained wrap technician with experience from the commercial signage industry, saw a gap in the Delhi market: genuine craftsmanship at a fair price. Most studios either over-promised and under-delivered, or delivered technically sound work with no attention to the finer details.

We started with car wrapping and quickly earned a reputation for doing what others wouldn't — wrapping door jambs completely, lapping edges properly, and never leaving a bubble or lifted corner. That reputation spread through referrals across Rohini, Pitampura, Shalimar Bagh, and eventually all of north Delhi.

"Detailing that defines presence."

— Core principle, AURAFX Studio

Today, AURAFX is a full-service automotive transformation studio. We've expanded to paint protection film, ceramic coatings, interior customisation, and custom builds — but the ethos remains unchanged. Every car gets the same attention, whether it's a Maruti Alto or a Toyota Fortuner.

We don't chase trends or upsell unnecessarily. We listen to what you want, assess your vehicle honestly, and recommend what genuinely makes sense for your car and your budget.

Our Journey

Six years of
steady growth.

2018

The Beginning

AURAFX opens in a 600 sq ft garage in Sector 8, Rohini. First three months focused exclusively on vinyl wraps and window films. Word spreads quickly through the local car enthusiast community.

2019

Adding PPF & Ceramic

After receiving repeated requests from customers for paint protection, we invest in PPF training and 3M/STEK certification. Ceramic coating services added to the menu. First major Toyota Fortuner full-PPF project completed.

2020–21

Expanding Through Uncertainty

Despite the challenges of the pandemic years, AURAFX maintains a steady stream of work through strong referral networks. Workshop expanded to 1,200 sq ft. Interior detailing and customisation services added.

2022

Custom Builds Take Off

Full custom build projects — combining wraps, chrome delete, alloy customisation, caliper painting, and interior work — become our most talked-about service. 400+ car milestone reached.

2023–24

Premium & Precision

800+ transformations completed. Body kit installation, colour-shift wraps, and advanced paint correction added to service list. Google rating consistently maintained above 4.9 stars.

2025

Today

AURAFX operates from a 2,000 sq ft dedicated workshop in Rohini. A team of five certified technicians handles everything from a quick chrome delete to a complete multi-day transformation — all to the same uncompromising standard.
