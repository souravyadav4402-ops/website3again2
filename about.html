The Foundation

Precision.
Protection.
Personality.

Three words. One mission.

AURAFX began in 2018 in a small workshop in Sector 8, Rohini. The founder, a trained wrap technician from the commercial signage industry, saw a gap in Delhi: genuine craftsmanship at a fair price.

We started with wrapping and quickly earned a reputation for doing what others wouldn't — wrapping door jambs completely, lapping edges properly, never leaving a bubble or lifted corner.

"Detailing that defines presence."

— Core principle, AURAFX Studio

Today, AURAFX is a full-service transformation studio. We've expanded to PPF, ceramic coatings, interior customisation, and custom builds — but the ethos remains unchanged. Every car gets the same attention, whether it's a Maruti Alto or a Toyota Fortuner.

We don't chase trends or upsell unnecessarily. We listen, assess honestly, and recommend what genuinely makes sense for your car and budget.

Our Journey

Six years of
steady growth.

2018

The Beginning

AURAFX opens in a 600 sq ft garage in Sector 8, Rohini. First three months focused on vinyl wraps and window films.

2019

Adding PPF & Ceramic

PPF training and 3M/STEK certification. Ceramic coating services added. First Toyota Fortuner full-PPF project completed.

2020–21

Expanding Through Uncertainty

Workshop expanded to 1,200 sq ft. Interior detailing and customisation services added despite pandemic challenges.

2022

Custom Builds Take Off

Full custom build projects — wraps, chrome delete, alloy customisation, caliper painting, and interior work. 400+ car milestone.

2023–24

Premium & Precision

800+ transformations completed. Body kits, colour-shift wraps, advanced paint correction added. Google rating maintained above 4.9.

2025

Today

AURAFX operates from a 2,000 sq ft workshop in Rohini. Five certified technicians handling everything to the same uncompromising standard.
