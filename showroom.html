Material Library

Every finish
we carry.

All materials are premium grade — 3M, STEK, and Avery Dennison certified stocks held at our workshop.

Gloss Black

Mirror-like deep black finish. High-impact, head-turning — the most popular wrap colour.

Matte Black

Flat, non-reflective black. Aggressive and stealthy with a distinctly modern edge.

Gloss Red

Vivid, saturated gloss red. High-gloss finish that catches every light.

Satin Blue

A subtle sheen between matte and gloss. Rich colour depth without full mirror reflectivity.

Chrome Silver

Mirror-effect chrome film. Maximum visual impact — available in gold, black chrome too.

Matte Army Green

Military-inspired flat green. Unique and rugged — increasingly popular on SUVs.

Colour Shift / Chameleon

Multi-tonal film that shifts hue with angle and light. Truly one-of-a-kind result.

Satin Pearl White

Clean, premium white with a subtle satin finish. Elegant and timeless on any car.
